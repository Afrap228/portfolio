About me

Afrah P

I build things people find.

I’m Afrah, a digital marketer who builds her own websites. I love the moment a clean design and a clear message land together, and a stranger becomes a customer.

I started in marketing, trained at the Institute of Digital Skills, and then moved into web development so I could build the pages my campaigns send people to. That mix means I can plan the message and also make the website that carries it.

I learn by making things: a fashion store site, an online academy site and an interactive 3D portfolio so far. Every project teaches me what people notice, what they ignore and what makes them click.

I’m a BBA graduate from Kannur University, based in Kasaragod, Kerala. I like clear plans over clever tricks, honest numbers over vanity metrics, and teams where people share what they know.

When I’m not building, I’m studying how brands talk to their customers: how a good caption, a simple page or a well-timed email makes someone stop and read.

Right now I’m looking for an entry-level role where I can learn fast, take real ownership of small wins and grow into bigger ones.
